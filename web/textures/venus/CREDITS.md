# Venus detail tiles – credits

`d5/{col}_{row}.jpg`: 640×640 pieces of the Venus surface (under the clouds), 22.5°
each (16 columns from longitude -180, 8 rows from the north pole).

Source: NASA/JPL, Magellan C3-MDIR radar mosaic
(`Venus_Magellan_C3-MDIR_Global_Mosaic_2025m`), level 5 tiles from
[NASA Venus Trek](https://trek.nasa.gov/venus/), public domain. Gaps in the
radar coverage were filled with a blur of their surroundings, and the tiles
were tinted at low frequency to the colours of `../planets/venus.jpg`.

Used by planet `detail` pictures (engine, globe.rs).
