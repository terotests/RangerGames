# Earth detail tiles – credits

`d5/{col}_{row}.jpg`: 768×768 pieces of the Earth, 22.5° each (16 columns
from longitude -180, 8 rows from the north pole), ~3.3 km a pixel (7 MB in all).

Source: NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble Next Generation with topography
and bathymetry, July 2004 (`world.topo.bathy.200407.3x21600x10800.jpg`,
Reto Stöckli, NASA/GSFC), public domain. Cut into 16 × 8 pieces and tinted
at low frequency, colour by colour, to `../planets/earth.jpg` so they blend
with it.

Used by planet `detail` pictures (engine, globe.rs).
