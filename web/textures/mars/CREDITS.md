# Mars detail tiles – credits

`d5/{col}_{row}.jpg`: 768×768 pieces of Mars, 22.5° each (16 columns from
longitude -180, 8 rows from the north pole), ~1.7 km a pixel (12 MB in all).

Source: NASA/JPL/USGS, Viking MDIM 2.1 colorized global mosaic
(`Mars_Viking_MDIM21_ClrMosaic_global_232m`), level 5 tiles from
[NASA Mars Trek](https://trek.nasa.gov/mars/), public domain. 4 × 4 tiles
were joined into each piece, made grey and tinted at low frequency to the
colours of `../planets/mars.jpg` so they blend with it.

Used by planet `detail` pictures (engine, globe.rs).
