# Mercury detail tiles – credits

`d5/{col}_{row}.jpg`: 640×640 pieces of Mercury, 22.5° each (16 columns from
longitude -180, 8 rows from the north pole).

Source: NASA/JHUAPL/Carnegie Institution of Washington, MESSENGER MDIS
basemap BDR mosaic (`Mercury_MESSENGER_MDIS_Basemap_BDR_Mosaic_Global_166m`),
level 5 tiles from [NASA Mercury Trek](https://trek.nasa.gov/mercury/), public
domain. The tiles were joined, made grey and tinted at low frequency to the
colours of `../planets/mercury.jpg` so they blend with it.

Used by planet `detail` pictures (engine, globe.rs).
