# Matka Marsiin: credits

- 3D models (space station modules, hull, astronauts), sounds and speech
  texts are made for this game in code: `tools/three/make-iss.mjs`,
  `tools/three/make-astronaut.mjs`, `tools/three/make-iss-sounds.mjs`,
  `tools/three/marsiin-voice.mjs`. The station's layout follows the real ISS
  (public NASA/ESA/JAXA/Roscosmos facts); no NASA images or models are used.
- The colours of the Earth and Mars globes are sampled from the planet
  textures in `web/textures/planets/` by Solar System Scope
  (https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), CC BY 4.0.
